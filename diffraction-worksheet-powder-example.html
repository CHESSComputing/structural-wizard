The diffraction worksheet collects the information needed to prepare for a successful beamtime. It helps guide experimental setup and data collection, allocate resources, and efficiently coordinate measurements across samples and techniques. Please complete it as thoroughly as possible.

The worksheet supplements, but does not replace, your detailed experimental plan. Your scientific and characterization goals, as well as all experimental timing, sequencing, and priorities, should be communicated in that plan.
